Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Policy explains how information may be processed when you visit Asuracomic and what choices are available to you.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the address you use and the information included in your message. We use it to review and respond to your request, maintain relevant correspondence, and protect the website.

Information processed automatically

Hosting and security providers may process technical records such as IP address, request time, requested URL, response status, browser type, device information, and referring page. These records may be used to deliver the website, investigate errors, prevent abuse, and maintain security.

Google Tag Manager and analytics technologies

The website loads Google Tag Manager. Tag Manager is a tool that can load measurement or other tags configured by the website operator. Depending on that configuration and your consent settings, those tags may process page URLs, referrers, browser or device details, interactions, cookies, or similar identifiers. Google and any configured service process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

You can learn more from Google's Privacy Policy. Browser settings and privacy tools may allow you to block or delete cookies, although doing so can affect website functionality.

How information is used

  • to provide, maintain, and secure the website;
  • to understand technical performance and diagnose errors;
  • to respond to messages, copyright notices, and support requests; and
  • to comply with legal obligations and protect rights.

Sharing

Information may be processed by hosting, security, email, and measurement providers when needed to supply their services. It may also be disclosed when required by law, to address fraud or security threats, or in connection with a business reorganization. We do not state that technical information is never shared because third-party infrastructure is used to operate the website.

Retention and security

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, subject to operational and legal requirements. No internet transmission or storage system is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may use browser controls to manage cookies and similar storage. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability. Requests can be sent to the contact address below; identity verification may be required.

Children's privacy

The website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

Changes and contact

We may update this Policy as the website or its data practices change. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to contact@asuracomic.cc.
