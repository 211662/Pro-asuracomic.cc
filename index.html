The Story Behind AsuraScans

Online comic readers commonly encounter several practical problems:

  • Domain changes can make the current website difficult to identify.
  • Intrusive advertising can interrupt reading and obscure navigation.

Asuracomic addresses these concerns with clear navigation, responsive layouts, searchable titles, and a single canonical domain.
